Privacy Policy

SRKB Unified · Last updated 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information the SRKB Unified app (“the App”), published by FiWpy, collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. Information the App handles

About attendees (entered by your centre’s authorised staff):

  • Name, mobile number, address and occupation
  • Which centre, batch and session they belong to, and their attendance
  • Records of calls made to them: outcome, remarks and follow-up dates

About app users (staff, callers, administrators):

  • Username, name and role, and login credentials
  • Their activity inside the App, such as attendance marked and calls logged

Technical information: the App version in use, IP address and request logs on our server, used to keep the service working and to investigate faults.

2. Information the App does not collect

The App requests internet and network-state access, plus the standard Android permissions that let its background sync run and resume after a restart (wake lock and receive-boot-completed). The direct-download (APK) version additionally asks for permission to install its own updates. Neither requests location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call-log permissions. The App contains no advertising. When you tap Call or WhatsApp, the App hands the number to your phone dialler or WhatsApp at your request; it does not read what those apps do.

3. Why we use it

  • To run attendance, batch, session and calling features for your centre
  • To keep records consistent across phones, including offline mode that syncs later
  • To upload your unsent attendance, registrations and call responses, and refresh the calling list and class data, in the background when a connection is available — with no notification and no tracking
  • To secure the service, find faults and prevent misuse

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

4. Where it is stored and who sees it

  • Data is stored on a server operated by the developer and is sent between your device and the server over HTTPS.
  • Your centre’s data is visible to authorised users of that centre, according to their role.
  • We do not sell personal data. It is shared only with the service providers listed in section 5, and where required by law.
  • For offline use, a copy of the data you are allowed to see is kept on your device. Cached copies are cleared when you log out; changes not yet sent to the server stay on the device until they sync; uninstalling the App removes its data. Android’s device backup may also keep a copy of app data in your Google account.

5. Service providers

  • Cloudflare carries traffic between the App and our server.
  • Google Sheets may receive attendance records where your centre has turned on spreadsheet export.
  • An AI assistant service used by administrators may process registration details such as names and mobile numbers to answer admin queries.

These providers process data only to deliver those functions.

6. How long we keep it

We keep records for as long as your centre uses the App, or until deletion is requested and completed. Server request logs (including the IP address of anyone who opens this website’s download page, because it checks the app version on our server) are kept for a limited period for troubleshooting.

7. Your choices — access, correction and deletion

If you are an attendee or a user and want your information corrected or deleted, or want to delete your account, email krsna6366@gmail.com with your name and registered mobile number, or ask your centre administrator. See also how to delete your account. We will act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

8. Security

Access requires a login and is limited by role and centre. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your credentials private and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

9. Children

The App is for use by centre staff, not for children. Any attendee records about minors are entered by your centre, which is responsible for having permission to do so.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
